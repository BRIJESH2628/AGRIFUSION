Government Agricultural Schemes
🌱
PM Kisan Samman Nidhi
₹6,000 per year direct benefit transfer to farmer bank accounts in 3 equal installments of ₹2,000 each.
Eligibility Criteria:
Open to all landholding farmer families with cultivable land in their names. Excludes: Institutional land holders, income tax payers in the last assessment year, and registered professionals (doctors, engineers, etc).
₹6,000/yr Support Official Portal ↗
💧
PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY)
Provides subsidies up to 90% for setting up micro-irrigation systems (Drip/Sprinkler) to promote water conservation.
Eligibility Criteria:
All farmers who own agricultural land and have access to a water source are eligible. High priority and maximum subsidy is given to small/marginal farmers and farmers belonging to SC/ST categories.
Up to 90% Subsidy Official Portal ↗
🛡
Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
Robust crop insurance scheme that protects farmers against drought, flood, and pest damage at heavily subsidized premiums.
Eligibility Criteria:
Available for all farmers growing notified crops in notified areas. Loanee farmers (those taking crop loans) are enrolled automatically by banks, while non-loanee farmers can opt-in voluntarily.
2% Premium for Kharif Official Portal ↗
